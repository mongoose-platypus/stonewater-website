Our Culture

Our people are our most valuable asset.

Our people are our most valuable asset — a fact that is reflected in our lean, flexible organization where thoughtful collaboration and meaningful relationships result in a fulfilling work environment.

Our culture is distinguished by its intellectual force, strong camaraderie, and openness to ideas. In our ongoing quest to operate an idea and human meritocracy, we highly value feedback at all levels and wholeheartedly celebrate each other’s achievements.

Our consultants thrive when they are both challenged and supported, making every engagement an opportunity to build skills, develop relationships, and deliver impactful work.

Career Path

Four levels, one meritocracy.

We help our clients accelerate growth and structure operations to support scalability. Our clients operate in fast-developing markets.

Associate

As an Associate at Stonewater, you provide strategic business advice to help organizations elevate their performance. You are tasked primarily with the collection of data and creation of client documents. Many a client problem arises from an absence of relevant data, so one of your main responsibilities is to interview knowledgeable parties and help formulate hypotheses. After sufficient inputs are gathered — typically via conversations with value-chain participants, interviews with industry specialists, quantitative surveys, and analysis of client data — the focus of effort turns to writing organized, concise, precise, and insightful recommendations.

You learn and apply a wide variety of skills such as presenting engagement findings, structuring research agendas, interviewing subject matter experts, and managing research analysts. You own select business development activities such as helping develop proposals and identifying potential prospects who would benefit from Stonewater’s capabilities.

Consultant

As a Consultant at Stonewater, you take responsibility for a project by working face-to-face with clients to diagnose complex problems and recommend data-driven solutions. You will lead associates and research analysts to ensure that the most practicable outcomes are delivered according to project plans. Consultants are responsible for multiple workstreams on larger engagements or are fully responsible for smaller engagements.

As integral members of the firm, Consultants act as role models for our Associates, onboarding new hires and mentoring others. You lead firm-building activities and internal training programs to expand your leadership skills and share your specific interests — board games, fitness, etc. — with colleagues. Consultants typically commence their role after mastering the Associate role, or enter as working professionals or advanced degree holders who seek to work with bright and curious colleagues.

Manager

As a Manager at Stonewater, you take a leading role in identifying, designing, and delivering improvement opportunities for the world’s leading organizations. You serve as the critical liaison between client needs and project-delivered solutions. During the initial stages of a project, you identify key questions or problems and develop an analysis and delivery structure for the engagement. You then oversee day-to-day management of the project itself, guiding a team of two to five consultants.

You constantly develop and apply your leadership, project management, client engagement, and problem-solving skills. In addition to project work, you help win new clients by publishing intellectual capital and contributing to project proposals. To our clients, you serve as an expert adviser on the many pertinent issues affecting their businesses and industries. To your fellow consultants, you are a proven leader who dependably steers projects toward success.

Principal

As a Principal at Stonewater, you create value not only for clients by delivering strategic advice, but also for Stonewater by driving business development and the creation of intellectual capital. You are primarily responsible for upholding the overall quality and integrity of client projects. Maintaining strong client relationships is crucial, so you need good interpersonal skills to nurture these relationships up to the board level.

Junior colleagues look to you as a role model as you motivate and guide them to deliver Stonewater’s strategic vision. You demonstrate thought leadership and develop subject matter expertise in your practice area to help clients solve complex business problems. You work closely with our partners to identify strategic new opportunities for Stonewater and master end-to-end business development activities from creating proposals to leading sales meetings.

Recruiting FAQ

Questions we are asked most.

Here are answers to the most common questions we receive about recruiting at Stonewater Partners.

How do I apply for a position at Stonewater Partners?

We are thrilled that you are interested in joining our firm and look forward to reviewing your application. In order to begin the recruiting process, we require candidates to provide a cover letter and resume. To submit your materials, please click here.

How long does the Stonewater recruitment process normally take?

The recruitment process is designed to hire individuals who are leaders among their classmates and will in the future be startup founders, investors, government policy makers, nonprofit leaders, and chief executive officers. We carefully consider each candidates’ individual strengths and weaknesses and take the necessary time — sometimes up to two months — to select individuals who can deliver notable results for our clients and build Stonewater’s presence across the globe.

What are the steps of the Stonewater recruiting process?

It is important to us to spend the time to learn about your unique talents, perspectives, and capabilities. In order to develop a complete picture, our recruitment process comprises of a writing and PowerPoint test followed by three interviews with Stonewater consultants and partners.

What is the purpose of the writing and PowerPoint test?

Our consultants tackle a wide variety of impactful and challenging questions on a daily basis. Thus, it is important for them to convey their thoughts in a structured, thoughtful, and polished manner across a variety of channels. This exercise tests the ability of applicants to internalize a question, develop a position, and convey it in two common formats:

MS Word: This output conveys the thoughts and concepts of the applicant in well-structured prose. This is the format used by Stonewater consultants to deliver white papers to central banks.

MS PowerPoint: This output presents the applicant’s position in a graphical and viewer-friendly slide presentation. This is the main format that Stonewater uses to discuss outputs and key initiatives with corporate leaders.

What do the interviews entail?

Each round of interviews includes two main components to varying degrees:

Fit questions that help us learn more about you and your background, interests, passions, and career aspirations.

Case questions that test your analytical, mathematical, and critical thinking skills.

Case interview practice materials are readily available, and we would recommend that you thoroughly familiarize yourself with the structure of case interviews before interviewing.

Must I travel to the Stonewater Partners offices for assessments and interviews?

We seek to attract the best and brightest consultants from across the globe. As a result, it is often impractical for us to conduct in-person interactions with candidates. Our recruiting process is designed to be geographically flexible, and allows for assessments and interviews to be conducted via email and video conference. Detailed instructions will be provided at every step of the recruitment process.

What does Stonewater Partners look for in a candidate?

While there is no rigid template for a successful Stonewater consultant, qualities that we evaluate include:

Critical Thinking: Solving problems is fundamental to our work, so we seek individuals who can frame complex issues and develop and communicate sound, realistic solutions.

Leadership: We believe that true leadership is rooted in open, reasoned, and thoughtful collaboration; we look for individuals that share these values.

Entrepreneurial Passion: As a young firm, we particularly value individuals with a proven record of proactively and passionately adding value outside standard responsibilities.

While many of our consultants have graduated from target schools, we frequently hire exceptional applicants from non-target schools as well.

I do not fit the traditional profile of a consultant. Should I still apply?

Stonewater consultants encounter a diverse range of impactful challenges on a daily basis, and we seek to attract talented individuals who come from similarly diverse backgrounds. Even if you feel that your experience is atypical, we would encourage you to apply to the position that you feel is most appropriate.

If I am rejected, can I reapply for a position?

We would ask that you leave a time period of one year between applications to allow additional experiences to be reflected on your resume.

Are you currently accepting applications for Associate Interns?

Stonewater accepts applications for Associate Interns all year round, and onboarding of Associate Interns is periodically scheduled.

What is the typical duration of an internship at Stonewater?

Associate Interns can expect to spend a minimum of 10 weeks and up to 6 months at Stonewater, though this is contingent on factors including academic obligations, business requirements, and scheduling.

Are internships at Stonewater full-time or part-time?

Associate Interns are expected to work full-time during their internship.

What will my working hours be if I am working remotely?

While Associates and Associate Interns can work remotely around the world, at least 6 of their working hours must fall between 10 a.m. and 7 p.m. Kuala Lumpur time.
