Case studies

Selected engagements.

Medtech

Resetting Brand Perception Across a Medtech Portfolio

A leading medical device company needed to know where its brand and commercial engagement stood with surgeons after a major market disruption. Stonewater Partners ran perception research across multiple specialties and markets to benchmark it against competitors and pinpoint exactly where share of voice was slipping.

Healthcare Investment

Building the Go-to-Market Case in a Fast-Growing Hospital Sector

A healthcare investor needed to know whether a fast-growing but fragmented hospital market was worth entering. Stonewater Partners built the demographic, disease-burden, and market fact base, from bed capacity to insurance penetration, to shape the go-to-market case and de-risk the investment.

Life Sciences

Charting Where to Play in Life Sciences Equipment

A life sciences equipment manufacturer needed to know which product lines and regions would drive its next wave of growth. Stonewater Partners mapped the market by segment and region, benchmarked the competitive landscape, and identified the strategic options worth pursuing first.

Surgical Robotics

Validating Demand for a Surgical Robotics Digital Platform

A surgical robotics company needed to know whether surgeons would actually pay for and use a new digital platform before it launched one. Stonewater Partners surveyed surgeons on their data practices, purchasing preferences, and appetite for digital tools, from case review to remote observation, to validate the launch case.
