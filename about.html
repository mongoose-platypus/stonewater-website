Who we are

What sets us apart.

Stonewater Partners is a global professional services firm that uses deep industry expertise and world-class research capabilities to deliver the strategic, tactical and operational solutions that drive on-the-ground improvements.

We are a boutique firm by choice. It keeps senior people on the work, decisions close to the client, and the distance between a recommendation and its implementation as short as possible. We're passionately committed to helping our clients and their customers thrive, working side by side to uncover valuable insights and deliver impactful results.

Client Impact

We measure success by the improvements our clients can see in their own operations.

Pragmatic Approach

Digital Infrastructure

Rebuilding Site Delivery Across a Multi-Market Tower Platform

The question
A tower platform growing through both build programmes and acquired portfolios could not tell whether delays came from its operating model or from difficult individual sites.
