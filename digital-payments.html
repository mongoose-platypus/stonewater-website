Case studies

Selected engagements.

Retail Banking

Beyond the Card: Mapping Retail Banking’s Next Growth Frontier

A client needed to see past payments and cards to find where retail banking is really headed. Stonewater Partners ran 100 interviews with senior bank executives across a dozen global markets to pinpoint the use cases and business models worth chasing next.

Acquiring

Pricing the Case for a New Acquirer Service

A client was eyeing a new servicing model to support its acquirer partners, but needed the fact base to price it. Stonewater Partners interviewed acquirers, including several of the largest banks in their markets, across multiple regions to quantify the cost, effort, and adoption case for the new offering.

Instant Payments

Charting the Next Wave of Instant Payments

As instant payment rails scaled worldwide, our client needed to know where its business could capture the next wave of growth. Stonewater Partners interviewed instant payment schemes, banks, and processors across several markets to map the use cases, enablers, and monetization models defining the category’s future.

Merchant Checkout

Cracking the Code on Checkout Adoption

Our client wanted merchants to embrace its new digital checkout experience, not just tolerate it. Stonewater Partners interviewed a cross-section of merchants across multiple retail sectors, alongside payment gateway partners, to uncover what would move them from interest to uptake.
