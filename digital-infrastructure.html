Turning site delivery into a measurable, repeatable operation.

What is growth actually costing you in speed, cost, and control? We help tower companies and passive infrastructure operators turn build programmes, colocation demand, and acquired portfolios into predictable delivery, documenting and measuring the processes that carry a site from a customer’s order through to revenue.

Case studies

Site Delivery

57% → 79% On-time colocation

Rebuilding Site Delivery Across a Multi-Market Tower Platform

A tower platform growing through both build programmes and acquired portfolios could not tell whether delays came from its operating model or from difficult individual sites. Stonewater Partners mapped and rebuilt the core operating chain across several markets, from colocation and maintenance to procurement and ground lease renewal, instrumenting each process so cycle time could be measured stage by stage. Trouble tickets closed within SLA rose from 76% to 97%, achieved while delivery volumes grew roughly eight-fold.

Finance Operations

9 Processes documented

Designing a Finance Operating Model for a Scaling Tower Platform

A tower platform whose organisation had grown up around its operations function had never documented the finance processes running alongside it, leaving ownership ambiguous and no shared calendar for budgeting and forecasting. Stonewater Partners catalogued nine finance processes across project-level workflows and corporate reporting, assigned ownership against the organisation chart, and built a single twelve-month calendar coordinating finance with five operating departments and the board.

14 → 5 days Lease slip generation

Making Order-to-Cash Visible Across Multiple Carrier Customers

A tower operator tracked each of its carrier customers differently, leaving order-to-cash cycle time invisible and revenue sitting in stages for which no department held clear responsibility. Stonewater Partners built a common process model for each carrier, reconciled milestone terminology and data sources across accounts, and stood up dashboards measuring every stage against target before handing the model to a trained in-house team.
